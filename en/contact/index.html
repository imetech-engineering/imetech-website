FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Many projects run partly or fully remote via video calls, email and shared documentation. I work with clients across the Netherlands. On-site visits are possible by appointment when needed. IMeTech Engineering is based in Aalten, Achterhoek.

What do I need for a quote?

A short description of your goal, any drawings or specifications, and desired timeline. No perfect dossier needed — a sketch or idea is enough to get started. After the first call you receive a clear quote.

How quickly do you respond to contact requests?

Usually within 24 hours on business days via email or the contact form. For urgent questions you can call directly at +31 6 12 38 54 39.

Which disciplines does IMeTech cover?

Software (embedded & PLC), electronics & PCB design, mechanical design and CAD, data science / machine learning and complete product development — one point of contact for the full picture, from idea to prototype.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes, the discovery call is always free and non-binding. We discuss your technical challenge, possible approach and timeline. Only after that you may receive a quote — with no obligations.
